### Portfolio Case Study (Copy-Paste)
* **Title:** Custom Cyberpunk Synth-Wave Theme for Tech Podcast & YouTube Series
* **Role:** AI Music Producer & Audio Engineer
* **Skills Used:** AI Music Generation, Audio Mastering, DAW Processing, Sound Design, Commercial Rights Clearing
* **Client Need:** A tech YouTube channel with 85,000 subscribers required an energetic, futuristic theme track for episodic intros and background beds. They faced frequent automated Content ID claims with stock music libraries and needed an exclusive track they owned outright.
* **Process:**
  1. Developed targeted prompts targeting driving 124 BPM synth-bass, gated drums, and modern analog lead textures.
  2. Exported raw multi-track stems and imported them into a DAW.

  4. Created seamless 15-second intro, 60-second bed, and 5-second outro cutdowns.
* **Result:** Client received a master WAV package with full stem separation and clear commercial licensing documentation, eliminating copyright claims and establishing a signature sonic identity.

## 30-Second Audio Preview Standards

Upwork allows direct audio uploads on Project Catalog listings. Use this structure to maximize conversion:

* **Target Loudness:** -14 LUFS integrated | True Peak ceiling: -1.0 dB
* **Format:** MP3 at 320 kbps (Stereo, 44.1 kHz)
* **Timeline Structure:**
  * **0:00 - 0:05:** Direct drop or main hook (no slow fades or silence).
  * **0:05 - 0:20:** Full instrumentation showcasing mix balance, bass control, and clarity.
  * **0:20 - 0:28:** Transition, vocal chop, or bridge showing arrangement skill.
  * **0:28 - 0:30:** Clean hit or natural acoustic/reverb tail ring-out.
